# 01.4 Financial Analytics & Yield Modeling

The **Financial Analytics & Yield Modeling Engine** powers the platform's capital allocation simulators and PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS) tenant isolation.

## Simulation Formulae
- **Annualized Wholesale APY**:
  $$\text{APY} = \left(\frac{\text{Net Profit per Cycle} \times \text{Annual Cycles}}{\text{Deployed Capital}}\right) \times 100$$
- **MAP Revenue Recovery**:
  $$\text{Recovered Revenue} = \text{Monthly Volume} \times \text{Price Erosion} \times 0.35 \times 0.88$$
