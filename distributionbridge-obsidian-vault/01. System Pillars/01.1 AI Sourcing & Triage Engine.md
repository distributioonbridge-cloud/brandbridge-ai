# 01.1 AI Sourcing & Triage Engine

The **AI Sourcing & Triage Engine** automates the ingestion, filtering, and valuation of wholesale product manifests directly against Amazon's live catalog.

## Key Capabilities
- **BuyBox Margin Evaluation**: Real-time comparison between wholesale supplier invoice pricing and active BuyBox winning offers.
- **FBA Fee Ingestion**: Automatic computation of referral fees (15%), pick/pack fulfillment fees, and variable weight surcharges.
- **Algorithmic Deal Score (0 - 100)**: Multi-factor ranking factoring velocity, gross margin, return on investment (ROI), and competitive seller saturation.
