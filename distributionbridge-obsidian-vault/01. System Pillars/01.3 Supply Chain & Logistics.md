# 01.3 Supply Chain & Logistics

The **Supply Chain & Logistics Engine** integrates 3PL cross-docking warehouses, automated pallet intake routing, and Amazon FBA Prep compliance.

## Active Warehouse Hub Network
- **Chicago Hub (MDW2)**: Optimized for Midwest and East Coast distribution. Base cross-dock rate: `$18.50 / pallet`.
- **Dallas Hub (DFW6)**: South-Central logistics gateway with bonded storage. Base rate: `$17.00 / pallet`.
- **Ontario Hub (ONT8)**: West Coast port-adjacent intake with automated labeling and carton prep.
