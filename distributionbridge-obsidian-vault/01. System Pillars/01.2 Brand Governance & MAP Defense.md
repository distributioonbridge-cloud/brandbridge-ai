# 01.2 Brand Governance & MAP Defense

The **Brand Governance & MAP Defense Engine** provides 24/7 autonomous surveillance over brand catalogs to eliminate unauthorized 3P sellers and enforce Minimum Advertised Price (MAP) compliance.

## Defense Workflow
1. **Surveillance**: Polls Amazon SP-API Competitive Pricing endpoints to detect pricing falling below established MAP thresholds.
2. **Threat Triage**: Flags rogue sellers, unauthorized hijackers, and grey-market distributors.
3. **Automated Enforcement**: Generates and dispatches cryptographically verified Cease & Desist (C&D) legal notices.
