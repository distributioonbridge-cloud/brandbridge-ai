# 02.5 Organization Settings Portal

The **Enterprise Settings Portal** manages organization configuration across 5 distinct operational tabs:
1. **General Profile**: Corporate credentials and notification endpoints.
2. **SP-API Integrations**: LWA Client ID, Merchant Token, and refresh cadences.
3. **3PL Logistics**: Default warehouse allocation (MDW2, DFW6, ONT8).
4. **Security & RLS**: Session keys and audit log exports.
5. **Stripe Billing**: SaaS subscription tiers (Seller Pro $199/mo, Brand Shield $499/mo).
