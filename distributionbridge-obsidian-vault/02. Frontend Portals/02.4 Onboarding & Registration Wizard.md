# 02.4 Onboarding & Registration Wizard

The **Registration & Onboarding Wizard** guides prospective enterprise users through identity verification, organization provisioning, and direct Amazon SP-API integration.

## 4-Step Funnel
1. **Organization Profile**: Entity Name, Admin Email, Password (PBKDF2 hashed).
2. **Account Role**: Wholesale Seller, Brand Registry Owner, or Capital Syndicate.
3. **Amazon LWA / SP-API OAuth**: 1-click authorization via [[amazon-callback.js]].
4. **Tenant Workspace Unlock**: Cryptographic session initialization and RLS partitioning.
