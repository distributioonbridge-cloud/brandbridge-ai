# 02.1 Next.js 14 App Architecture

The frontend is constructed with **Next.js 14 App Router**, **React 18**, and **Tailwind CSS**, featuring dynamic state tracking, theme variables, and modular sub-routing.

## Route Map
- `/`: Primary Matrix Landing Page (see [[02.2 Landing Page & 3D Projection Matrix]] & [[landing-page-v5.tsx]])
- `/saas`: Sidebar-driven SaaS Console (see [[02.3 Enterprise SaaS Console & Bento Grid]] & [[saas-portal-v3.tsx]])
- `/register`: 4-Step Onboarding Wizard (see [[02.4 Onboarding & Registration Wizard]] & [[register-page-v2.tsx]])
- `/settings`: Enterprise Configuration Center (see [[02.5 Organization Settings Portal]] & [[settings-page-v2.tsx]])
- `/seller`: Wholesale Sourcing Dashboard
- `/brand`: Brand Registry Defense Portal
